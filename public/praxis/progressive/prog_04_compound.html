Compound Growth & Effective Rates

Learn by doing. Start with one year of simple growth and progressively move toward compounding frequencies, effective annual rates and solving for the rate.

Core concept: Growth compounds: FV = PV · (1 + r/m)m·t with m periods per year. More frequent compounding raises the effective annual rate (EAR) above the nominal rate.

1Level 1 — One Year, Simple

BeginnerGrowth

Challenge: What is printed?

def main():
    var pv = 100.0
    var r = 0.10
    print(pv * (1.0 + r))
One year and no compounding — just multiply by 1 + r and read off the result.
Solution 
110.0

100 grows at 10% for one year: 100 × 1.10 = 110.0.

2Level 2 — Two Years of Compounding

Beginner+Compounding

Challenge: What is printed?

def main():
    var pv = 100.0
    var r = 0.10
    print(pv * (1.0 + r) ** 2)
The square applies to the whole growth factor, not just the principal — last year's interest earns interest too.
Solution 
121.00000000000001

Interest earns interest: 100 × 1.10² = 121 (floating point may show 121.00000000000001).

3Level 3 — Quarterly Compounding

IntermediateFrequency

Challenge: 100 at a nominal 8% compounded quarterly for one year — what is printed (2 decimals)?

def main():
    var pv = 100.0
    var r = 0.08
    var m = 4
    print(pv * (1.0 + r / Float64(m)) ** Int(m))
Convert the nominal rate to a per-quarter rate first, then raise that growth factor to the fourth power.
Solution 
108.24321600000002

Each quarter earns 2%: 100 × 1.02⁴ ≈ 108.2432. Quarterly compounding already beats the annual 108.00.

4Level 4 — Effective Annual Rate

Intermediate+EAR

Challenge: What is the EAR of a nominal 10% compounded semiannually, in percent (2 decimals)?

def main():
    var r = 0.10
    var m = 2
    var ear = (1.0 + r / Float64(m)) ** Int(m) - 1.0
    print(ear * 100.0)
Two half-year periods each grow by 5%. Compound them, remove the original 1.0, and express the result in percent.
Solution 
10.25

(1 + 0.05)² − 1 = 0.1025, i.e. an effective annual rate of 10.25% — the nominal 10% understates what you actually earn.

5Level 5 — Doubling Time

AdvancedLogs & Rule of 72

Challenge: At 10% annual interest, roughly how many years to double (Rule of 72)?

def main():
    var rate_percent = 10.0
    print(72.0 / rate_percent)
No powers needed — the Rule of 72 has one division, and the rate is already given as a percent.
Solution 
7.2

The Rule of 72: doubling time ≈ 72 / rate%. At 10% that is ≈ 7.2 years (the exact value ln 2 / ln 1.1 ≈ 7.27).

6Level 6 — Expert Challenge

AdvancedCode Reasoning Modification

Challenge: You need the annual rate that turns 100 into 150 in 5 years. Complete the line using powers — recall x**(1/5) is the fifth root. What rate (percent, 2 decimals) is printed?

def main():
    var pv = 100.0
    var fv = 150.0
    var t = 5.0
    var r = (fv / pv) ** (1.0 / t) - 1.0   # your line
    print(r * 100.0)
Raise the growth factor to the one-fifth power, then subtract the original 1.0 — the 1 that represents the starting amount.
Solution 
8.447177119768946

Invert FV = PV(1+r)t: r = (FV/PV)1/t − 1 = 1.50.2 − 1 ≈ 8.45% per year. Solving for the rate is the inverse of every compounding level before this one.
